Terms

Draft. These are short, plain terms for now. They will be replaced after a legal review before the first community uses the app.

Not an emergency service

TrailSafe is a community safety tool. It helps members of a trail community look out for each other. It does not replace calling emergency services. In an emergency, always call 112 as well.

No guarantee

TrailSafe depends on your phone, your signal and other members being nearby. We cannot promise that an alert will reach anyone, or that anyone will be able to help. You go out at your own risk.

Communities

You join a community with a community join code from its leader. Leaders approve and can remove members. Be respectful, and do not send false alerts.

Free to use

TrailSafe is free for you and your community. We may change or stop the service, and we will tell you if we do.

Law

These terms follow the laws of South Africa.
