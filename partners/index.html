For coffee shops

Win the loyalty of your local trail community.

One coffee shop per trail community.

Members who earn a free coffee come to you to redeem it, and your coffee is covered at cost once a corporate sponsor is secured. A trail community that knows where to find a friendly coffee shop becomes regulars.

  1. Write to us. Tell us your coffee shop and the trail community near you.
  2. We set you up. You get your own private page. There is no app to install and no sign-in.
  3. A member shows you a code. It is five digits and works once.
  4. Type it in. When you see “Coffee confirmed”, hand over the coffee.

For local businesses

Sponsor the free coffee for your trail community.

A business, such as a local grocery store, that would like to help keep the trail community safe and earn its loyalty. You pay for the free coffees at cost price, and every member who earns one sees your name.

  1. Write to us. Tell us your business and the trail community you would like to support.
  2. We agree the details. With you, and with the coffee shop in that community. The money is arranged directly, outside the app.
  3. Members earn free coffees for helping make the trails safer.
  4. You are seen. Your name appears wherever a member sees their free coffee.

Write to us about sponsoring
