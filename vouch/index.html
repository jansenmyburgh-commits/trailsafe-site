‹ TrailSafe

Someone has asked to join your community.

TrailSafe is a community trail safety app. Knowing where fellow trail users are makes the trail safer for everyone. Every new member is let in only when members who know them vouch for them, so the people on your map are people your community knows.

Already on TrailSafe?

Open TrailSafe, tap the menu, then Waiting to join. Check the last digits of their number against the one in your contacts, or message them on the number you have and ask if they asked to join. Then tap I know them, vouch.

Only vouch for people you know. If something doesn’t feel right, tap Raise a concern: only TrailSafe sees it.

Not on TrailSafe yet?

Get the free app, join your trail community, and you can vouch for the people you know.
