Free coffee for making the trails safer.

Every hour you are out and helping your community stay safe counts toward your free cup of coffee. Your trail community’s coffee shop and a local sponsor make it possible, and every member who earns a coffee sees who sponsored it.

  1. Go out on the trails. Open TrailSafe, tap Start, and the time you spend on your trails counts.
  2. Watch your mug fill. A little mug on the map shows your progress.
  3. Redeem it. When it is full, show your code at your community’s coffee shop.

TrailSafe is free for you and your community. Coffee shops and sponsors are set up community by community, so the coffee arrives in your trail community once its coffee shop and sponsor have joined.
