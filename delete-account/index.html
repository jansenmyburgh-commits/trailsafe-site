Delete your TrailSafe account.

You can delete your account in the TrailSafe app. Deleting your account can’t be undone.

Everything we hold about you is deleted straight away. The one thing that stays for a short time is an alert you sent: its entry in your community’s alert log remains until 7 days after the alert ended, without your name.

  1. Open TrailSafe.
  2. Open the menu (≡), then Your profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then confirm.

No longer have the app? Email jansen@trailsafety.app from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We will delete it and reply to confirm.
