Privacy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

TrailSafe is a community trail safety app. A trail community that knows where other community members are is a safer community. This page says what we keep, who can see it, and how you stay in charge of it.

What we keep about you

  • Your email address, your first name and last name, and whether you are a runner, hiker or cyclist.
  • Which community you belong to, and a record that you accepted how TrailSafe works.
  • Your choice about showing or hiding your location when you head out.
  • Your phone’s notification address, so TrailSafe can send you alerts.
  • A running total of the time you have spent out on the trails, used only to work out your free coffee. Only the TrailSafe back-end team can see it. We also keep the 5-digit code you ask for when you redeem a coffee, until it has been used or has expired.
  • Anything you send us yourself, such as a bug report, a feature request, a coffee shop suggestion or a request to move to another community. If you attach a screenshot, we keep that too. Only the TrailSafe owner and developer team see these, and only to improve the app.
  • If something goes wrong inside the app, the app sends us a short report so we can fix it: what failed, where in the app, the app version, and your phone’s model and system version. It never includes your name, email, account or position. Only the TrailSafe developer team sees these reports.

We keep your email address and name for as long as you are on TrailSafe.

Your location

TrailSafe uses your location only while you are out, after you tap Start. We keep your latest position only, not a list of where you have been. When you stop, it is removed. We keep no location history.

Visible only to approved community members, and only while you’re out.

You can choose to hide your location. Members still know you’re out nearby, but can’t see where you are. Hidden locations are never displayed with a name or any other identifying detail. On the Dashboard map they appear only as anonymous points.

Other communities. Approved members of other TrailSafe communities within 20 km of you can see you as a dot on the map while you are out. They never see your name. Your own community sees your name, unless you have hidden your location.

Looking from home. When you open the Dashboard to see how many community members are out near you, TrailSafe uses a single position from your phone to find the right area. We do not store it.

Trail reminders (optional)

Trail reminders are off unless you turn them on in Your profile.

If you turn them on, your phone needs location "Allow all the time" (Android) or "Always" (iPhone). Your phone itself notices when you reach one of your trail areas and reminds you to press Start. This happens on your phone. It does not send your location to TrailSafe, and TrailSafe is not told when a reminder was shown. The only thing your phone asks us for is the list of trail areas for your region, which is not personal.

You can turn reminders off at any time in Your profile, or take away the permission in your phone’s settings.

Alerts

When you send an alert, members within 5 km are told, and see where you are. People in your own community also see your name. Members of other communities see only that a member of your community has issued an alert. It also appears in your community’s alert log for 7 days, including who sent it, where it started, when, and how it ended. Each entry is deleted 7 days after it ends.

TrailSafe is not an emergency service. In an emergency, always call 112 as well.

Who helps us run TrailSafe

We use these companies to run the app. Each one only handles what it needs to do its job.

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in, hosted in Ireland.
  • Resend: sends you your email verification code.
  • Google Firebase: sends notifications to Android phones.
  • Apple: sends notifications to iPhones. (applies once the iPhone app is released)
  • Cloudflare: a short check that you are a person, when you sign in or sign up.
  • Google: if you choose to sign in with Google, Google tells us your name and email address and that it is really you. We receive nothing else from your Google account, and Google learns that you signed in to TrailSafe.

Our maps are served from our own files, so no map company receives your location. We do not sell your information and we do not show advertising.

Your choices

  • Show or hide your location each time you start, or ask the app to ask you every time.
  • Turn trail reminders on or off.
  • Stop sharing at any time by tapping Stop. Your position is removed.
  • Delete your account in the app: open the menu, then Your profile, then Delete my account. Everything we hold about you is deleted straight away, and it can’t be undone. The one thing that stays for a short time is an alert you sent: its entry in your community’s alert log remains until 7 days after the alert ended, without your name.
